Ebola outbreak status

Ebola virus disease, including disease caused by Bundibugyo virus, leads to severe regional outbreaks. It spreads through bodily fluids and has not caused a global pandemic. This page lists matching WHO Disease Outbreak News items and CDC travel notices from today’s snapshot.
