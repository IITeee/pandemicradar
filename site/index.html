Field notes

Three plagues

Sporeveil

A violet bloom in the soil. It spreads one tile at a time across grass and paths while you remain in that place. It never chases. Standing in it fills the infection meter. A cloth mask halves that. Step onto clean ground and the meter eases.

Rushfever

A fever of sprinting. Infected people patrol, burst toward you when they can see you, then slump and rest. They do not stain the ground. A touch costs health and infection. The mask does not stop a tackle. Break their line of sight, move while they rest, or dash through the burst.

Amberhaze

Luminous fog that travels a fixed route. It does not chase and it does not stop. Overlap fills infection quickly. It is slower than you. Wait in a gap, or dash a thin bank.

The road

Dr. Lumen in Ashfen keeps a cloth mask. Whisperwood hides the Rust Key, which opens the Old Mill storeroom. Glassport's clinic holds the Cord Key. The field lab, east of the cordon, holds the Haven Seal. The seal opens Safe Haven, south of the cordon.

Field Tonics cut infection. Supplies restore health. At a full infection meter, the road ends.
